13 POSIX, ESP32-C3, and cloud

Same portable C services. Three execution targets. Full markdown: docs/architecture/.

Application and services must not call pthread, FreeRTOS, or ESP-IDF. Those stay in OSAL, HAL, and drivers. CAN IDs are prototype/simulated. Not ISO 26262 or WP.29 certified.
